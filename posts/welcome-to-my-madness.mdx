# Welcome to My Madness

![Chris on the set of Queer Shorts 2019](/images/QSPortrait.png)

Well, guess I'm doing this. Are blogs even still a thing? I don't know, but here I am. In all seriousness I wanted to get whatever thoughts out of my head and on "paper" somewhere. It helps me process things, especially with my ADHD; and why not get my thoughts and ideas out there.

Allow me to introduce myself. I go by Riku (I'll cover why in another post), but my real name is Chris. I'll answer to either, but I prefer Riku in non-professional, extracurricular spaces. I currently reside in Madison, WI, but get to Chicago so often folks tend to think I live/moved there.

In my normal, everyday life, I work in Higher Education Technology as a de facto Digital Product Manager in Student Affairs. It's been an interesting experience, especially given it being a career change from my original industry of Video Game Development, but I love it. It's a unique role in our office, so it definitely keeps me on my toes.

Outside of work, I travel, play TTRPGs and video games, and am heavily involved in the LGBTQ+ Leather and Furry scenes. I'm also involved in theatre when I can, usually in the stage management role. In the TTRPG space, I stream one of the games I run, "Reign of Vecna", as part of Halfling Wizard Productions on [Youtube](https://www.youtube.com/@Halfling_wizard_productions) and [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/halfling_wizard_prod).

You'll find posts from professional to personal, spanning various topics. Fair warning, there will be some _spicier_ topics, as I am heavily involved in the leather and kink communities, but there is a toggle to hide those posts, or blur just the images of the posts (No nudity, but some kinkwear/activities may be pictured). Expect posts as I feel like it, but if you want to keep up with me on the day-to-day, my social links can be found at [riku.gay](https://riku.gay/).

That's it for now. See y'all on the other side!
